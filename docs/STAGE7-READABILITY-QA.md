# Stage 7 Readability QA

Date: 2026-08-18

Commercial QA found that several supporting texts in the Stage 7 explainer were rendered at 8–9 px. The conversion layer works functionally, but that size is too small for comfortable mobile review.

This change raises supporting copy and interactive controls to a more readable baseline without changing the funnel, CTA destination, lead behavior, EN/ES logic, disclaimers, or outreach state.

QA intent:
- preserve Stage 7 EN/ES contract;
- improve mobile scanning and comprehension;
- keep the section compact;
- no new claims or guarantees;
- no outreach authorization.
