LOCAL LEAD FORGE
Sales FAQ

Clear answers before you decide.

Local Lead Forge helps local service businesses capture and organize qualified opportunities through a conversion-focused web experience and bilingual lead intake. These answers describe the current founding-client offer without promising specific business results.

What does Local Lead Forge do?
LLF provides a conversion-focused web and lead-capture system designed to help local service businesses turn more website visitors into qualified opportunities. The current service includes setup, bilingual intake, lead delivery, hosting/operation, maintenance, and ongoing technical support within the agreed scope.
Does the system work in English and Spanish?
Yes. The current lead-intake experience is designed to support English and Spanish so visitors can provide the information your team needs in either language.
What happens after a visitor submits a lead?
The system captures the configured lead details and routes them to the destination approved during onboarding. Before activation, LLF performs a controlled test and requires proof that the test lead reached the approved destination.
Does LLF guarantee leads, appointments, sales, rankings, or revenue?
No. LLF improves the website lead-capture and delivery process, but traffic, prospect behavior, response speed, market conditions, pricing, sales performance, and other factors remain outside LLF's control. No specific number of leads, appointments, sales, rankings, revenue, or ROI is guaranteed.
How much does the founding-client package cost?
The current founding-client reference is $299 for initial setup plus $199 per month for the ongoing service. Final billing, scope, renewal, and cancellation terms are controlled by the customer-ready agreement and checkout disclosures in force when a customer purchases.
What is included in setup versus the monthly service?
Setup covers the initial business configuration, agreed content and intake configuration, lead routing, testing, and launch preparation. The monthly service covers hosting/operation, workflow maintenance, reasonable in-scope content changes, lead-delivery monitoring, technical checks, support, and reporting as rolled out under the agreed service scope.
Can LLF work with an existing website?
Often, yes. The exact implementation depends on the website platform, plan, access model, and technical constraints. LLF prefers delegated or least-privilege access when available and can use an owner-assisted installation path when direct access is not appropriate.
Will you need my main password?
Not when a safer delegated or collaborator option exists. LLF's standard is minimum necessary access. Passwords, banking details, SSNs, recovery codes, and other sensitive credentials should not be placed in onboarding forms, shared Drive documents, or source code.
How long does implementation take?
Timing depends on the agreed scope and how quickly required business information, approvals, lead destination, and any necessary access are available. LLF does not treat the project as ready to activate until required QA and a controlled lead-delivery test pass.
What support and maintenance are included?
The monthly service includes ongoing operation and reasonable support within the contracted scope. Larger redesigns, unrelated software work, paid advertising, complex third-party integrations, and other out-of-scope work are not automatically included unless separately agreed.
How are changes and cancellations handled?
In-scope changes are handled through the normal support process. Cancellation timing and any billing consequences follow the customer-ready agreement in force for the account; LLF does not rely on an informal “cancel anytime” promise.
How does LLF protect quality before launch?
The launch process uses evidence gates: verified business facts, configuration review, build/type checks where applicable, desktop/mobile QA, bilingual behavior checks, forms and navigation checks, and a marked end-to-end test lead that must be proven received at the approved destination before activation.
