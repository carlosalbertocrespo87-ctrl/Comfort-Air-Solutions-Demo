# Local Lead Forge — Client #1 Handoff Checklist v1

Status: INTERNAL OPERATING SOURCE

Purpose: bridge commercial acceptance, onboarding, activation, and post-launch support without improvisation.

## A. Commercial acceptance — before payment
- [ ] Customer/legal business identity confirmed.
- [ ] Authorized decision-maker/contact confirmed.
- [ ] Standard launch pricing confirmed: $299 one-time setup + $199/month.
- [ ] Scope and exclusions acknowledged; no guaranteed lead/job/revenue claims.
- [ ] Customer-facing agreement/order-form/privacy acceptance gate passed.
- [ ] Live payment is explicitly authorized and technically available before any real charge.

## B. Routing and factual handoff — before configuration
- [ ] Primary lead-delivery destination confirmed.
- [ ] Backup destination recorded if applicable.
- [ ] Person responsible for factual approval identified.
- [ ] Services, service area, hours, emergency handling, phone/email and allowed claims verified.
- [ ] Required brand assets/materials received.
- [ ] No passwords, API keys, banking data, SSN or identity documents collected through normal intake.

## C. Demo-to-production integrity
- [ ] Correct company identity throughout; no data from another demo/client.
- [ ] Demo is clearly non-affiliated/private until approved.
- [ ] Demo CTA routes to LLF lead capture, not an unapproved clickable prospect phone number.
- [ ] Any prospect-specific QR resolves to that prospect's exact private demo URL.
- [ ] QR and primary demo button resolve to the same approved destination.
- [ ] Desktop/mobile and EN/ES paths pass where enabled.

## D. Activation gate
- [ ] Customer factual validation completed.
- [ ] Final test lead received at the configured destination.
- [ ] Domain/location for live system confirmed.
- [ ] Support channel communicated.
- [ ] Activation date recorded.
- [ ] Monthly-service start date recorded and communicated.
- [ ] Temporary/delegated accesses reviewed and revoked when no longer required.
- [ ] No live routing/activation occurs until all applicable gates pass.

## E. Post-launch follow-up
- [ ] 24–48 hours: verify lead delivery, obvious errors and customer access/support understanding.
- [ ] 7 days: review lead quality/flow, routing friction and factual corrections.
- [ ] 30 days: value-focused review; leads captured, response workflow, useful outcomes, friction and prioritized improvements.
- [ ] Record changes requested and classify as maintenance vs scope expansion before committing work.

## Stop conditions
Do not accept live payment, activate live routing, or represent a customer fact as verified when its corresponding gate is incomplete. Customer-specific facts and channels must be approved before production use.
