# Local Lead Forge — Reply-to-Close System v1

Status: INTERNAL SALES PLAYBOOK

## Principle
When a prospect replies, answer the question they actually asked, keep momentum, and move toward one clear next step. Do not oversell, invent urgency, guarantee outcomes or overwhelm with technical details.

## Response patterns

### "Interested" / "Tell me more"
Thanks for taking a look. The idea is simple: we configure the lead-capture experience for your business, test it across desktop/mobile and English/Spanish, and route the requests to the destination you approve. The next step is a quick review of what would be included for [Company] and the setup details.

### "How much?"
Our standard starting offer is $299 for setup and $199/month for the ongoing service. Before activation we confirm exactly what is included for your business so there are no surprises.

### "What is included?"
The setup covers the business-specific configuration, lead-capture experience, English/Spanish setup where applicable, routing configuration and pre-launch QA. The monthly service covers the hosted/maintained lead-capture system and normal operational maintenance within the agreed scope. Anything outside the agreed scope is confirmed before work is added.

### "Does this replace my website?"
Not necessarily. The lead-capture system can be positioned as an additional conversion path rather than forcing a full website replacement. The exact deployment depends on the business and the agreed setup.

### "How does the AI work?"
For the customer, it behaves like a guided service-intake assistant. It gathers the problem, location, urgency and contact details in a controlled flow. It is configured with guardrails so it does not promise pricing, appointments, technician availability, warranties or other facts the business has not approved.

### "Will this get me more leads/sales?"
The system is designed to make it easier for website visitors to submit a service request, including people who do not call first. We do not guarantee a specific number of leads, jobs or revenue because results depend on traffic, market conditions and the business's follow-up process.

### "Call me"
Acknowledge and arrange the call only if the owner is available/comfortable. If text/email is preferred internally, request a suitable time and keep the preparation concise.

### "Not interested"
Thanks for letting me know. I appreciate you taking a look. We will not continue outreach unless you contact us again.

### "Who are you?"
I’m Carlos with Local Lead Forge. We build and maintain lead-capture systems for local service businesses. The page you received is a private, unofficial demo we prepared specifically to show the concept using publicly available business information; it is not presented as your official site.

## Closing progression
Preferred sequence:
1. Prospect expresses interest.
2. Answer immediate question.
3. Confirm scope/value in simple terms.
4. Confirm standard price when appropriate.
5. Use the pre-payment gate.
6. Once payment is legitimately approved, enter Client #1 onboarding.

## Safety
- Never claim a payment succeeded without provider evidence.
- Never activate live routing or billing before the relevant gate passes.
- Never promise lead volume, revenue, ROI or appointment availability.
- Never send additional cold outreach merely because this playbook exists; outbound email remains separately authorization-gated.
