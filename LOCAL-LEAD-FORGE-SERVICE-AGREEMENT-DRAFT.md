## 2. SERVICES

Local Lead Forge will provide the Client with an AI-assisted website lead capture and qualification service.

The initial service may include:

- website lead-capture assistant;
- HVAC lead qualification flow;
- collection of visitor name and phone number;
- collection of HVAC issue;
- collection of city / ZIP code;
- collection of preferred timing;
- English and Spanish support when included;
- delivery of qualified lead information to an approved Client email address;
- reasonable configuration and maintenance of the installed lead-capture system.

The exact configuration may vary depending on the Client's website platform and technical environment.
