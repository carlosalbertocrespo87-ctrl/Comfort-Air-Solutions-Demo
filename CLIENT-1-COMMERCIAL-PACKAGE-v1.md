# Local Lead Forge — Client #1 Commercial Package v1

Status: INTERNAL/CUSTOMER-FACING SOURCE OF TRUTH

## Standard starting offer
- Setup: $299 one-time.
- Ongoing service: $199/month.

## Setup includes
- Business-specific configuration using verified facts supplied/approved by the customer.
- Lead-capture experience configuration.
- English/Spanish configuration where included and supported.
- Approved lead-routing destination setup.
- Desktop/mobile functional QA.
- EN/ES QA where enabled.
- Form/assistant flow QA.
- Pre-activation customer approval gate.

## Monthly service includes
- Hosted/maintained lead-capture system within agreed scope.
- Normal operational maintenance of the configured system.
- Lead-routing configuration maintenance within agreed scope.
- Reasonable QA when supported configuration changes are made.

## Not automatically included
Unless explicitly agreed, the standard package does not promise:
- ad spend or paid media management
- SEO rankings
- guaranteed lead volume
- guaranteed jobs, revenue or ROI
- 24/7 human answering service
- CRM replacement
- unlimited website redesign work
- custom integrations outside the standard supported scope

## What happens after a prospect says yes
1. Confirm business identity and decision-maker.
2. Confirm exact scope and pricing.
3. Pass the pre-payment gate.
4. Complete legitimate payment flow when available/approved.
5. Send onboarding intake.
6. Validate business facts, services, service area and routing.
7. Configure the customer version.
8. Run QA.
9. Obtain approval.
10. Activate live routing/version only after all activation gates pass.
11. Follow up after launch and collect value-focused feedback.

## Customer-facing positioning
Local Lead Forge provides a managed lead-capture layer for local service businesses. The purpose is to make it easier for potential customers to submit a structured service request, including visitors who prefer Spanish or who do not call first.

## Core safeguards
- No unsupported claims.

- No live payment, routing or activation without the corresponding gate.
